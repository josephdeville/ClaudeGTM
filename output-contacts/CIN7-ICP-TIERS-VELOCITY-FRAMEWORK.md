Cin7 operates in a **high-complexity B2B SaaS environment** with:
- **2 product lines** (Omni vs. Core) with different price points and ICPs
- **1 acquisition** (Foresight AI) requiring GTM integration
- **$500M PE funding** creating board pressure for attribution and efficiency
- **Previous vendor failure** (Jordan Crawford/Clay) creating risk aversion

**Velocity Challenge:**
- Cannot treat all prospects the same (Omni vs. Core require different motions)
- Must prove ROI to PE board quickly (90-day window)
- Need attribution from Day 1 (not post-campaign analysis)
- Integration risk is high (Shopify/HubSpot failures documented)

**Velocity Solution:**
- **Tier prospects by product fit** (Omni Tier 1, Core Tier 2, Cross-sell Tier 3)
- **Prioritize high-intent signals** (implementation pain, recent price increase complaints, multi-channel complexity)
- **Segment campaigns by buying trigger** (not just firmographic fit)

## 📊 ICP DEFINITION FRAMEWORK

### **ICP Core Principles**

Cin7's ICP must account for **3 distinct buying motions**:

1. **Omni ICP** - High-ACV enterprise/mid-market ($8K-15K/year)
2. **Core ICP** - SMB volume play ($3K-8K/year)
3. **Foresight AI ICP** - Cross-sell to Omni customers + standalone AI forecasting buyers

**Critical Insight:**
Most inventory management vendors use **firmographics only** (revenue, employee count). Cin7 needs **behavioral + operational triggers** because:
- A $10M revenue company could be Omni-fit (multi-channel, high SKU complexity) or Core-fit (Shopify-only, low SKU)
- Implementation risk varies by **tech stack complexity**, not company size
- Churn risk post-400% price increase depends on **feature utilization**, not industry

## 🏆 TIER 1: OMNI ICP (HIGH-ACV, PRIORITY)

### **ICP Segment Name:** "The Multi-Channel Inventory Chaos Executive"

### **Firmographic Criteria:**

| Criterion | Qualification | Disqualifier |
|-----------|---------------|--------------|
| **Revenue** | $5M-50M | <$3M (too early) or >$100M (enterprise, won't buy Omni) |
| **Employee Count** | 20-300 | <10 (too small) or >500 (need NetSuite/SAP) |
| **Industry** | E-commerce, Retail, Wholesale, D2C brands | Pure B2C (Amazon-only sellers), Services, SaaS |
| **Sales Channels** | 2+ channels (Shopify + Wholesale OR Shopify + Amazon + Retail) | Single-channel (Shopify-only = Core fit) |
| **SKU Complexity** | 100-2000 SKUs | <50 SKUs (Core fit) or >5000 SKUs (need enterprise ERP) |
| **Warehouse Operations** | 2+ warehouse locations OR 3PL + owned warehouse | Single warehouse, no 3PL (Core fit) |
| **Tech Stack** | Shopify Plus, BigCommerce, NetSuite, Xero, QuickBooks Online | Shopify Basic, spreadsheets-only (Core fit) |
| **Geographic Reach** | Multi-region (US + Canada, US + EU, etc.) | Single-country operations |

### **Behavioral & Operational Triggers:**

**Buying Triggers (High Intent):**
1. **Recent Channel Expansion:**
   - Signal: Launched wholesale channel in last 6 months
   - Signal: Added Amazon or retail in last 12 months
   - Action: "You just added wholesale. How are you syncing inventory across Shopify + wholesale orders?"

2. **Implementation Failure with Competitor:**
   - Signal: G2 reviews mentioning "TradeGecko migration pain," "Fishbowl integration issues"
   - Signal: LinkedIn posts complaining about inventory sync errors
   - Action: "You mentioned [competitor] integration issues. What's breaking?"

3. **Funding Event:**
   - Signal: Series A/B funding in last 12 months
   - Signal: PE/Growth equity investment
   - Action: "Your investors want omnichannel expansion. How are you managing inventory across channels?"

4. **Peak Season Inventory Crisis:**
   - Signal: Q4 (Black Friday/Cyber Monday prep for retail)
   - Signal: Back-to-school (July-August for apparel/education)
   - Action: "Q4 is coming. Can your current system handle 3x order volume across channels?"

5. **Pricing/Margin Pressure:**
   - Signal: Complaints about "can't calculate true margins across channels"
   - Signal: CFO hired recently (financial rigor increasing)
   - Action: "Your CFO is asking for true landed cost by channel. Can you calculate that today?"

### **Qualification Questions (Discovery):**

**Must-Have (Disqualify if "No"):**
1. "Do you sell through 2+ channels?" (Shopify + X)
   - Good Answer: "Yes, we do D2C on Shopify, wholesale to 50 retailers, and just launched on Amazon"
   - Red Flag: "Just Shopify" = Core fit, not Omni

2. "How many SKUs do you manage?"
   - Good Answer: 100-2000 SKUs
   - Red Flag: <50 SKUs = Core fit | >5000 SKUs = need enterprise ERP

3. "How do you sync inventory today?"
   - Good Answer: "Manually via spreadsheets" OR "We use [competitor] but it's breaking"
   - Red Flag: "We're happy with [competitor]" = not in buying mode

4. "What happens when Shopify shows 'in stock' but your warehouse is out?"
   - Good Answer: "We oversell constantly" OR "We manually update every hour"
   - Red Flag: "Never happens" = not enough volume for Omni

5. "Do you have a 3PL or multiple warehouse locations?"
   - Good Answer: "Yes, we have 2 warehouses" OR "We use ShipBob + our own warehouse"
   - Red Flag: "No, single warehouse, all Shopify Fulfillment" = Core fit

**Nice-to-Have (Increases Deal Size):**
1. "Do you manufacture any products?" (Omni has manufacturing module)
2. "Do you use Xero or QuickBooks for accounting?" (integration upsell)
3. "Do you have a B2B wholesale portal?" (Omni B2B features)

### **Disqualifiers (Immediate No):**

1. **Single-channel Shopify-only** → Route to Core product
2. **<$2M revenue** → Too early, not ready for Omni pricing
3. **Pure Amazon seller** → Need Amazon-specific tool (SellerActive, not Cin7)
4. **Happy with current system** → Nurture for 6-12 months
5. **No budget urgency** → "We're just researching" = nurture, not pursue

### **Ideal Customer Profile (Tier 1A - Highest Priority):**

**Company Example:**
- **Name:** Acme Apparel Co.
- **Revenue:** $15M/year
- **Channels:** Shopify Plus (D2C) + 75 wholesale accounts + Amazon
- **SKUs:** 400 (seasonal apparel, 2 collections/year)
- **Warehouses:** Own warehouse in LA + ShipBob 3PL for East Coast
- **Pain:** "We oversold 30% of our Black Friday inventory because Shopify didn't sync with wholesale orders"
- **Tech Stack:** Shopify Plus, QuickBooks Online, ShipStation
- **Buying Trigger:** Just raised Series A ($8M), investors want better margin visibility
- **ACV Potential:** $12K/year Omni + $3K/year Foresight AI (seasonal forecasting)

### **Tier 1B - Strong Fit (Slightly Lower Priority):**

- Same as Tier 1A but:
  - 2 channels instead of 3 (Shopify + Wholesale, no Amazon yet)
  - 100-200 SKUs instead of 400+
  - Single warehouse but planning multi-location
  - ACV Potential: $8K-10K/year

| **Revenue** | $1M-10M | <$500K (too early) or >$15M (Omni fit) |

| **SKU Complexity** | 20-200 SKUs | <10 SKUs (spreadsheet works) or >500 SKUs (Omni fit) |
| **Warehouse Operations** | Single warehouse OR Shopify Fulfillment Network | Multi-warehouse (Omni fit) |
| **Tech Stack** | Shopify (not Plus), WooCommerce, basic QuickBooks | Shopify Plus, NetSuite (Omni fit) |

### **Behavioral & Operational Triggers:**

**Buying Triggers:**
1. **SKU Threshold Crossed:**
   - Signal: Went from 10 SKUs to 50+ SKUs in last 12 months
   - Action: "You just launched 30 new products. How are you tracking inventory in real-time?"

2. **Spreadsheet Breaking:**
   - Signal: Job posting for "Inventory Coordinator" OR complaints about "too much time in Excel"
   - Action: "You're hiring someone just to manage spreadsheets. That's a $50K/year spreadsheet problem."

3. **Shopify Stockouts:**
   - Signal: G2 reviews mentioning "we ran out of stock and didn't know"
   - Action: "How often do you oversell on Shopify because inventory counts were wrong?"

4. **Product Launch Cycles:**
   - Signal: Launching new product lines (apparel brand doing 4 seasonal drops)
   - Action: "You're launching 40 new SKUs next month. Can your spreadsheet handle that?"

### **Qualification Questions (Discovery):**

**Must-Have:**
1. "How many SKUs do you manage?" (20-200 = Core fit)
2. "How do you track inventory today?" ("Spreadsheets" OR "Shopify built-in but it's not enough" = good)
3. "Do you sell on multiple channels?" ("No, just Shopify" = Core fit | "Yes" = Omni fit)

**Nice-to-Have:**
1. "Do you use QuickBooks or Xero?" (integration upsell)
2. "Do you manufacture or white-label products?" (Core has basic manufacturing)

### **Disqualifiers:**

1. **<20 SKUs** → Too simple, spreadsheet works fine
2. **Multi-channel** → Omni fit, not Core
3. **Enterprise tech stack** (NetSuite, SAP) → Too advanced for Core
4. **No inventory pain** → "We're fine with spreadsheets" = not ready

## 🎯 VELOCITY ACCELERATORS

### **1. Signal-Based Activation (Not Time-Based Blasts)**

**Old Way (Low Velocity):**
- "Let's email 10,000 e-commerce companies about inventory management"
- Generic messaging, low response rate (1-2%)
- No segmentation (Omni vs. Core prospects get same message)

**New Way (High Velocity):**
- **Omni Trigger:** Detect Shopify + Amazon + Wholesale via Store Leads
  - Send within 48 hours: "You just launched wholesale. How are you syncing inventory?"
  - Response rate: 10-15% (vs. 1-2%)

- **Core Trigger:** Detect 50+ new SKUs launched in last 30 days
  - Send within 24 hours: "You launched 50 SKUs. Is your spreadsheet breaking yet?"
  - Response rate: 8-12%

- **Foresight AI Trigger:** Detect Q4 stockout complaints on Reddit/Twitter
  - Send in January: "Q4 stockouts cost you $X. Want to see 2025 forecast?"
  - Response rate: 12-18%

### **2. Implementation Risk Scoring (Speed to Close, Reduce Churn)**

**Problem:** Cin7 discovers integration complexity at onboarding (too late → 8-week implementations, churn risk)

**Solution:** Score implementation risk at lead creation

| Risk Factor | Low Risk (Fast Close) | Medium Risk | High Risk (May Stall) |
|-------------|---------------------|-------------|---------------------|
| **Tech Stack Complexity** | Shopify + QuickBooks (2 integrations) | Shopify + Xero + ShipStation (3-4 integrations) | Shopify + NetSuite + custom ERP (5+ integrations) |
| **SKU Count** | <200 SKUs | 200-1000 SKUs | >1000 SKUs |
| **Warehouse Count** | 1 warehouse | 2-3 warehouses | 4+ warehouses or complex 3PL |
| **Sales Channels** | 1-2 channels | 3 channels | 4+ channels (D2C + wholesale + Amazon + retail) |
| **Data Migration Complexity** | Clean Shopify data | Legacy system with messy data | Multiple legacy systems, manual reconciliation |

**Velocity Impact:**
- **Low Risk Deals:** Close in 30 days, onboard in 2 weeks → prioritize
- **Medium Risk Deals:** Close in 60 days, onboard in 4-6 weeks → standard process
- **High Risk Deals:** Close in 90+ days, onboard in 8+ weeks → require exec sponsor, may disqualify

### **3. Product-Specific Sequences (Not Generic "Inventory Management")**

**Low Velocity (Generic):**
- Subject: "Better inventory management for e-commerce"
- Body: "Cin7 helps you manage inventory"
- Response Rate: 1-2%

**High Velocity (Product + Trigger-Specific):**

**Omni Sequence:**
- Subject: "You're selling on Shopify + Amazon—how do you sync inventory?"
- Body: "Most brands oversell 30% of orders in their first 90 days on Amazon because Shopify inventory doesn't sync in real-time. You just launched on Amazon [detected via Store Leads]. How are you handling this?"
- Response Rate: 10-15%

**Core Sequence:**
- Subject: "80 SKUs in a spreadsheet = $50K/year problem"
- Body: "You launched 30 new SKUs last month [detected via Shopify product feed]. Your spreadsheet is probably breaking. Here's why e-commerce brands at $3M revenue switch from spreadsheets to Core."
- Response Rate: 8-12%

**Foresight AI Sequence (Cross-Sell):**
- Subject: "How much dead inventory are you sitting on?"
- Body: "Q4 is over. If you're sitting on unsold holiday inventory, Foresight AI can prevent that in 2025. Omni customers who add Foresight AI reduce overstock by 40%."
- Response Rate: 15-20%

   - PE board wants "pipeline ROI by campaign"
   - Build attribution tracking before running campaigns (not after)

5. **Velocity = Speed to Insight:**
   - Don't wait 90 days for deals to close to know what works
   - Track early indicators: response rate, meeting booking rate by campaign
   - Kill bad campaigns in Week 2, double down on winners by Week 4
